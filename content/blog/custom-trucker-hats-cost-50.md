# How Much Do 50 Custom Trucker Hats Cost?

There is no single published all-in price for 50 custom trucker hats with a logo: the total depends on the hat specification, decoration and delivery destination. NanCrown quotes trucker hats to the actual design on an EXW basis, with shipping excluded, so request the hat, sample and delivery costs separately before setting your budget.

## Start with a complete cost, not just the cap price

A useful budget begins with the quoted cost of the finished hats, including the agreed logo treatment. Add the physical sample and its shipping, then add the cost of moving the bulk order to your destination. Ask your carrier or customs adviser about any applicable duties, taxes and clearance charges as well. These destination costs depend on the shipment and are not included in an EXW factory quote.

EXW means the quoted price is based on the goods being made available at the named factory location. It is not a delivered price: transport and related arrangements need to be agreed separately. When comparing offers, check the named location, the scope of transport and who is responsible for each charge. A lower cap price can be misleading if it leaves more costs outside the quote.

NanCrown's minimum is 50 pieces per style, colour and size. For a small first order, specify a single style, colour and size if that is what you need; do not assume a mix of colours or sizes can be supplied below the minimum for each. The [trucker hat page](/custom/trucker-hats) is a useful starting point for describing the construction you want.

## What changes the factory quote?

The quote is based on the actual build. For a trucker hat, describe the front panels and mesh, the colours you want for each part, the brim and the closure. A reference photo helps communicate the silhouette, but a photo alone may not show fabric weight, interior finishing or fit. Tell the factory which details matter to your customer and ask it to identify the materials in the quote.

The logo is another part of the specification. Send the artwork, desired dimensions, position and colours, and say whether you want embroidery, a patch or a print. Fine lettering, small gaps and intricate shapes deserve particular attention: the artwork may need adjustment to remain legible when made on fabric. A mockup helps with placement, but it cannot fully show the texture or stitching of a finished logo. Review the physical sample before approving the artwork for production.

Special fabrics, processes, hardware and packaging are quoted separately. If you want branded packaging or a particular closure, include that request at the start and ask whether it is included in the quoted configuration. Conversely, if you are trying to stay within a target budget, explain which elements are essential and where you are open to alternatives. The factory can then quote an actual specification instead of guessing at one.

## Include the sample in your budget

A standard physical sample with a single logo costs US$50, excluding shipping. Additional logo positions and complex designs are quoted separately. The sample fee is refunded when an order reaches 1,000 or more pieces of the same design; do not assume that refund applies to a 50-piece order.

A sample is a decision tool as well as a cost. Check the crown shape on a head, how the mesh joins the front, the brim shape and the closure. Look closely at logo placement, lettering, thread or patch edges, and the relationship between the logo and seams. Compare colours in suitable lighting and check any requested label or packaging details against the written specification.

From a production perspective, ambiguity is a common source of rework. For example, a logo approved as a flat image can look different once stitched onto a curved front panel; a closure that looks right in a photo may feel different when worn. Mark corrections clearly and agree on the final sample and specification before bulk production. Ask how a requested change affects the quote and schedule before giving approval.

## Account for delivery and timing

For the all-in figure, ask for the bulk shipment cost to your actual destination and make sure the quote identifies the delivery service and the charges it covers. If a separate carrier handles the shipment, provide the factory quote and shipment details to that carrier and ask what additional charges may apply. Include sample shipping separately, since the sample and bulk order are different shipments.

Sampling takes 7–15 days depending on style and requirements. Bulk production normally takes 25–30 days after sample approval, with shipping time additional; confirm the schedule for your specific order. If you have an event or launch date, work backwards from the date the hats must arrive and allow time for artwork confirmation, sample review, any revisions, shipping and destination clearance. A factory production estimate alone is not an arrival date.

## What to send for a useful quote

A clear request makes it easier to compare costs and avoids a quote for the wrong hat. Include:

- The quantity, style, colour and size, with a reference for the desired trucker shape.
- Front-panel and mesh colours, preferred materials, brim and closure requirements.
- Logo artwork, dimensions, position, colours and preferred decoration method.
- Any label, tag or packaging requirements, or confirmation that standard options are acceptable.
- Your delivery destination, required arrival date and whether you need shipping quoted.
- Your target budget, if you have one, and the features you can adjust.

Ask for the hat quote to state what is included, plus separate sample and shipping charges. The [pricing page](/pricing) explains the published reference pricing and terms; its standard cotton-twill cap reference is not a trucker-hat quote. Keep the written specification alongside the sample approval so both sides can check the same details when the order moves into production.

## FAQ

### Does the minimum apply to each colour and size?

Yes. NanCrown's minimum applies per style, colour and size. Confirm your intended breakdown when requesting the quote.

### Is the logo automatically included in an all-in price?

There is no published all-in trucker-hat price. Identify the logo method and placement in your request, then check that the written hat quote includes the agreed decoration.

### Can I calculate the delivered cost from the factory quote alone?

No. EXW quotations exclude shipping. Obtain the relevant transport and destination charges for your shipment before calculating a delivered budget.

### Should I approve a mockup instead of a physical sample?

A mockup is useful for discussing artwork and placement, but review the physical sample for the actual fit, materials and decoration before approving bulk production.
